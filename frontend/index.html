Upload transactions

Upload a CSV of transactions in the PaySim format and every row is scored by the fraud model. Up to 5,000,000 rows or 500 MB. Big files are processed in the background with a progress bar. Uploads are private to this browser, and the scored file is kept for 24 hours.
